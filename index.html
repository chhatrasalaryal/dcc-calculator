Illustrative calculator. The markup is measured over the reference rate you enter; scheme rules require the cardholder to choose freely between the two currencies, with the rate and markup shown on screen and receipt, and EU/UK rules require the markup over the ECB rate. Real provider contracts may define rebates as a % of DCC volume rather than a share of markup: a rebate of R% of volume equals a markup share of R ÷ markup.
