# DCC markup & commission calculator

Live page: https://chhatrasalaryal.github.io/dcc-calculator/

A single-page calculator for dynamic currency conversion (DCC):

- DCC rate offered and what the cardholder pays, versus paying in local currency with their bank's FX fee
- Split of the DCC markup between the merchant, Global Payments and the DCC provider, with scheme/processing costs borne by any party or passed to the cardholder
- Monthly and annual revenue projection, and a markup sensitivity table

Preset exchange rates are illustrative; enter the current reference rate before relying on results. No data leaves the browser.
